# Frozen 0.1.6 validation

The small development sample supports the cost premise: economical models produced useful work at substantially lower model-priced cost. It also exposed a review defect and an unresolved recovery gate. This is not a held-out benchmark or a broad savings claim.

The [protocol](FROZEN_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL_20260921.md) was committed before live calls. Runtime 0.1.6 stayed unchanged across every run. All briefs were public or invented. No model result was rerun to obtain a better score.

## Original ownership error

A controlled probe supplied the original flawed PDF and a synthetic initial Luna Low attempt. Jev detected the unsupported owner at 0.80. Its recovery judgment assigned reasoning failure 0.79, below the 0.90 gate. DUKE blocked before invoking a repair worker. The PDF remained unchanged. Detection worked; the complete repair loop remains unproven. This seeded probe is excluded from the natural cost comparison.

## Review sample

Six correct/flawed pairs covered ownership, arithmetic, conditional dates, survey attribution, labeled proposals, and required sections. Jev detected all six planted defects and falsely failed none of the six correct outputs. Only one correct output passed; five stayed unverified. Two separate missing-context cases also stayed unverified. Uncertainty is not an artifact failure, but it limits automatic verification.

## Natural task comparison

Four clear briefs covered code, research, writing, and a PDF. Inputs, tools, skills, roster, and runtime matched. Artifact assessment was separate from Jev and the workers, but was not blinded. Original coding assertions and additional edge cases passed for both modes; research sources and writing constraints were checked, and both PDF pages were rendered and inspected.

Three automatic routes used the economical rules/fallback path; the PDF was a direct Jev selection. These results therefore describe DUKE automatic mode, not four successful Jev model judgments.

The automatic PDF needs a small structure and ownership-clarity revision under the strict rubric. It places an explicitly unrun pilot under Completed and names the loan-register owner beside verification activity without naming that role clearly. Its arithmetic and main facts are preserved. Treating this borderline artifact as acceptable changes automatic acceptance to 4/4 and cost per acceptable artifact to $0.007329. It is not classified as a critical factual failure.

The automatic coding artifact was correct, but Jev falsely rejected its statement that verification passed. The focused response review had omitted the actual verifier receipts. This is a workflow defect, separate from model output quality. All other final natural-task reviews remained unverified.

The proxy is approximately 97% lower per strictly acceptable artifact in this sample, including all recorded reviews and failed workflow attempts. At the stricter acceptable-and-completed boundary, automatic cost is $0.014659 per result, approximately 95% lower. The price scenario is frozen from September 20; it is not a current price lookup, subscription bill, or invoice reconciliation. All observed weekly subscription-window deltas rounded to zero, so this sample cannot quantify allowance savings.

## Evidence and next action

[Machine-readable results](evidence/frozen-validation-20260921.json) include receipt hashes, usage, routing, review outcomes, artifact hashes, and cost boundaries. [Independent review](evidence/frozen-016-independent-review.json) records the rubric decisions. The [automatic PDF](evidence/frozen-016-artifacts/jev-status.pdf) and [Astra PDF](evidence/frozen-016-artifacts/strong-medium-status.pdf) are retained unchanged.

The immediate fix is to supply bounded, task-specific verifier observations to response review. Keep missing execution evidence neutral. Do not lower the recovery threshold to make this probe pass. After that fix, evaluate recovery judgments separately with known causes and outcomes before changing recovery policy. A larger held-out comparison still needs independently assessed quality and whole-task cost accounting.
